A make.fun entry for **Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown**: one job title,
60 characters at most, the weirdest one we could think of. A blind judge
compares titles two at a time and picks the weirder, funnier one.

## The title

> Lead Tooth Fairy Claims Adjuster

A real job ("Claims Adjuster") with one gloriously odd twist (the claims
come from the Tooth Fairy). It sounds like a real job, pictures one person
at a desk processing tiny claims for lost teeth, and lands on first read.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, deterministic) writes the title to
`/output/entry.txt`. It validates the text against the bounty gate first:
1–60 characters, UTF-8, not only whitespace.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
